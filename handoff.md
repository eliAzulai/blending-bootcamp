## Current Decision To Preserve

The June 30 strategy spec says cohort 1 launches as **grades 3-6**, because that is Ilana's teaching strength and the easier Zoom sell.

Do not silently change the launch band back to ages 6-8 just because many existing WordPets assets fit that younger band.

Instead, preserve this tradeoff:

- Grades 3-6 is the launch band.
- Existing app/comics/KB assets are mostly early-reader / age-7 / CVC oriented.
- Therefore the "near-zero curriculum" claim is retired for cohort 1.
- Term 1 needs fresh, age-appropriate authoring.
- The current Zoom landing page must be re-copied away from "Ages 6-8."
- The app is optional/light support until its content fits the band, unless Eli/Ilana explicitly decide to age it up first.

## Core Question

Answer this:

**How do we fill, prepare, and run one fixed-time WordPets grades 3-6 online cohort while reducing Ilana's scheduling and context-switching load?**

## Required Output

Create a document with these sections.

### 1. Executive Decision

Give the recommended first-cohort plan in one paragraph:

- band,
- offer,
- enrollment window,
- minimum-to-run threshold,
- first operating milestone.

Clearly mark anything that still needs Eli/Ilana confirmation.

### 2. The Offer

Define:

- who the group is for,
- the reading-stage problem,
- what parents are buying,
- what the WordPets app does and does not promise,
- what is excluded.

Keep the offer age-appropriate for grades 3-6 strugglers. Avoid babyish early-reader framing.

### 3. Human Decisions Needed

List decisions still needed before launch:

- price,
- term length,
- group size cap,
- minimum paid commitments,
- candidate time slots,
- private-lesson monthly income,
- per-school monthly income,
- whether cohort 1 uses the app lightly or waits for age-up content,
- whether trial is free, paid-refundable, or deposit-first.

### 4. Enrollment Probe

Design the smallest structured intake that can cluster demand.

It must capture:

- child's grade and age,
- reading-stage proxy,
- candidate time-slot choice,
- parent contact,
- paid-commitment readiness.

Give exact form questions and answer options.

### 5. Landing Page Change Brief

Specify the smallest changes needed for `marketing/zoom-classes/index.html`.

Required changes:

- remove/rewrite "Ages 6-8" framing,
- replace free-text mailto/WhatsApp as the primary CTA,
- add the structured probe,
- keep WhatsApp as secondary contact only,
- avoid overclaiming that the app is ready for grades 3-6.

Do not redesign the whole page unless a source proves that is necessary.

### 6. Warm-Family Sanity Check

Write 2-3 WhatsApp scripts Eli/Ilana can send before broad promotion.

The scripts should test:

- would a family consider a fixed-time group instead of private/ad-hoc work,
- which time slots are realistic,
- whether the grades 3-6 reading-support offer is clear.

Keep the tone direct, local, and not salesy.

### 7. Response Clustering Workflow

Define Eli's workflow:

- collect responses,
- group by reading stage,
- group by viable time slot,
- identify first cluster that reaches the paid-commitment threshold,
- hand Ilana one clean cohort list and one proposed class time.

Name what Eli keeps away from Ilana: open inbox, one-off demos, per-family scheduling negotiation, and unclustered parent replies.

### 8. Launch Clock And Go/No-Go

Set a 2-3 week enrollment clock.

Define:

- interest,
- paid commitment,
- minimum-to-run,
- go rule,
- no-go rule,
- what to do if no cluster fills.

Use placeholders where Eli/Ilana must choose numbers:

- `[N paid commitments]`
- `[date]`
- `[price]`
- `[candidate slots]`

### 9. Term 1 Prep Brief

Define what Ilana must prepare before class 1.

Rules:

- one band,
- one term,
- front-loaded prep,
- no authoring-while-teaching as the launch plan,
- if Term 1 cannot be prepared in time, move the start date.

Use KB/app/comics assets only as inspiration where age-appropriate. Do not treat them as enough for grades 3-6.

### 10. Wean Math

Create a simple worksheet:

- `cohort revenue = price x paid students`
- `monthly cohort revenue = monthly price x paid students`
- `cohorts to replace private income = private monthly income / monthly cohort revenue`
- `cohorts to replace school income = school monthly income / monthly cohort revenue`

Mark all income numbers as human inputs.

### 11. Risks And Kill Criteria

List risks:

- no paid cluster forms,
- families reject fixed-time group,
- grade spread creates too much level spread,
- Term 1 authoring is too large,
- app mismatch causes confusion,
- Ilana gets dragged into admin.

For each risk, give a mitigation or a kill criterion.

### 12. Proof Trail

End with:

- source files used,
- major claims marked `Sourced`, `Inference`, or `Human decision needed`,
- unresolved conflicts,
- open questions for Eli/Ilana.

## Review Standard
