# Preview Words Before Reading

Before a child reads a decodable book or passage, show them the words they'll encounter — organized by sound pattern and with sight words clearly marked.

## How the Whitebooklets do it

The last page of each book (which Ilana reviews before starting) contains:
- "My Words" — complete word list organized by sound pattern
- Sight words marked with asterisk (*)
- Sound families grouped together (e.g., Ss section, -at word family)
- "New high frequency words" called out explicitly

Example from Book #11 "Dad and Sam":
- Sight word: *said
- Ss words: Sam, sand
- -at family: sat

## Why preview matters

"That's like really cool to like review even before you start reading the book — you know which [words] are gonna be used." The child isn't ambushed by unfamiliar words mid-story. They've already seen and practiced the challenging ones.

## Application to WordPets

Before a Read Aloud or Phonics session:
- Show a "word preview" screen with the session's target words
- Mark sight words distinctly (different color or icon)
- Group words by sound pattern
- Let the child tap each word to hear it pronounced
- Then start the actual reading/practice

This reduces frustration and builds confidence before the real work begins.
