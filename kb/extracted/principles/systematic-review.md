# Systematic Review of Prior Letters

Every new letter book must include a systematic review of all letters learned so far. This is not optional — it's one of the strongest features of the Letterbook series.

## Why it matters

Children forget quickly. Each new book in the Letterbook series reviews every letter that the kids had learned thus far. By the fourth book, they're reviewing A, B, C, and D together. This spiral approach means no letter is ever "done" — it keeps getting reinforced.

## How it works in the source material

- The Letterbook for D includes sorting/categorization activities for all prior letters (A, B, C, D)
- Kids drag items to the correct letter section (e.g., anteater → A, desk → D)
- This is embedded naturally in the activities, not as a separate "review quiz"

## Application to WordPets

In the app, every new phonics session should include quick review touches on previously learned sounds. Don't just teach /d/ in isolation — include a few /a/, /b/, /c/ items in the same session. The review should feel like part of the activity, not a test.

## Source quote

"It's really important — what I like about this series is it does a review, a systematic review of every letter that they learned so far. Every book, they review all the letters."
