## Page 1

Write-a-thon Summary  
Welcome to the first ever Gush Adumim English Write-a-thon, and likely in all of Israel! You have 5 
weeks (starting Dec 28-Feb 2) to write, write, and write! 
Types of written assignments- each one must be completed in order to reach the next tier, but 
once Level 4 has been reached, you can make multiple submissions under the same category of 
your choice. Work should be checked by a teacher or parent before the final submission. Email to: 
ilanahecht@gmail.com or hand-in as a hard copy. Assignments can be hand-written or typed. For 
the longer assignments, it is preferable to type them. If you need a sample of a category to help 
give you ideas and to see the format, just ask.  
You will have an impressive portfolio of writing to share by the end of this competition! 
Level 1: 
Poetry- Diamond Poem/Cinquain Opposites (separate instructions see below) 
Poetry- Acrostic (separate instructions see below)  
Fact and Opinion Chart- make a chart about a subject of your choice and include 5 facts and 5 
opinions 
Level 2: 
Travel Diary/Daily Journaling- write a few sentences per day summarizing your travels or 
experiences, include images 
Descriptive Writing- choose an image of a person, place, animal, natural scenery etc and write a 
very descriptive paragraph about the image. Make sure to use lots of adjectives, and answer 
questions about the image like who, where, what, when, why and how.  
Level 3: 
Advertisement- sell a service or product, include graphics/images, can be real or imaginary- 1 
page flyer 
Non-Fiction- Science Research Project (Animals, Planets, etc) 2-3 pages, include images and 
facts about a chosen subject of interest 
Historical Biography- choose a famous person from history and provide a one-paged summary of 
their life, try to include a portrait of that person 
Level 4:  
Short story, fiction or true story- 2-3 pages, you can submit multiple short stories if you don’t want 
to do a comic strip or play 
Comic strip- minimum 9 columns, speech bubbles and images, can be hand-drawn or vectors 
from the internet 
Play- write a short play with 1-3 acts, make sure to include multiple characters and determine the 
plot and setting

Instructions for poetry

Set-up: 
Step 1- Come up with a pair of opposites that are things (noun-antonyms) 
Step 2- Draw 7 lines (or leave space for 7 lines) 
Step 3- Write the first word in the first line, and the second word in the last line (7th line) 
Write/Create: 
Line 1- first word of your opposite pair 
Line 2- 2 words which describe line 1(adjectives) 
Line 3- “ing” words (verbs), action words ending in ing, 3 words describing line 1 
Line 4- 2 things connected to line 1, and 2 things connected to the word in line 7  
Line 5- 3 “ing” words, (verbs) action words describing the word in line 7 
Line 6- 2 words which describe line 7 
Line 7- second word of your opposite pair 
Acrostic: 
Each letter of the word written vertically needs a start a full sentence or at least phrase with 
multiple words. Try to use alliteration when you can (each word starts with the same letter, see line 
6-8 below) For example: 
Can turn your frown upside down 
Happily found in every home 
Oasis, that’s what you are 
Count the days until I see you again 
One of a kind 
Lovely, leaves you longing for more 
Absolutely amazing  
Tempting, terrific and tasty 
Excellent choice for a gift
