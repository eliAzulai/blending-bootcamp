## Page 1

Animals
 
1. What animal best represents you? Why?
 
2. What creature scares you? 
 
3. Except for food, do humans need other animals? Give some examples.
 
4. What is the most effective way to save endangered species?
 
5. What traits have made humans a successful species?
 
6. What are some animals you don’t know much about?
 
7. What is the cutest animal you can think of? How about the ugliest?
 
8. Do you have a pet or did you in the past? Why or why not? 
 
9. If you could own any exotic pet, what would it be?
 
10. What values can we learn from animals?  
For example we can learn loyalty from the dog, perseverance from ants, hard work from the ox, etc
 
11. What do you think about animal research for medications etc? Is it morally acceptable?
