Ilana — before I brought you 28 questions, I sat with one for a long time. There were two honest answers, and they pointed to different lives. This is the fork I carried into our conversation — and how your own words resolved it.

The one question

What I was actually asking

If WordPets never became a 10,000-family subscription app — would you still be happy if it became a smaller product, plus literacy groups, plus premium coaching, and you never returned to school teaching?

Two answers, two lives

Path 1

Replace the school system

Founder first · teacher second.

In this world
  • You stop teaching live as soon as income allows
  • WordPets becomes a standalone product business
  • Zoom groups are temporary scaffolding
  • Private lessons end
  • Income comes from subscription scale — thousands of families at $7–10/month
What it requires
  • Patience — years, not months
  • Serious tech build & retention metrics
  • Marketing growth as a full job
  • Runway while subscription scale compounds
Honest read Biggest ceiling. Highest variance. You'd be a founder full‑time, not a teacher.
Path 2

Redesign your teaching life around it

Entrepreneurial educator.

In this world
  • You keep teaching — on your terms, not the school's
  • Zoom literacy groups replace school hours (₪3,200–4,800/month from 2–3 groups)
  • Private coaching stays, priced as expertise
  • WordPets is the brand, the funnel, the digital backbone, a passive income layer
What it generates
  • Immediate income, from week one
  • More control, less dependency on scale
  • Lower stress, lower identity risk
  • Optionality — can tilt toward Path 1 later if traction appears
Honest read Lower ceiling, sturdier floor. Most successful education founders never fully leave this path.

Your answer — the pivot

When I asked you which one, you typed

i think i would be path 2 until path 1 becomes a reality

— Ilana, from our planning chat
That's the most psychologically stable founder answer there is. Not lack of ambition — strategic sequencing. Path 2 generates income now. Path 1 waits for proof. If the proof never comes, Path 2 is already the dream many successful education founders never leave.

How it resolves into the roadmap

Phase 1 · Now

Live teaching primary

Zoom literacy groups & private lessons as the income engine. WordPets ships a tight pilot — 5 of your students + our 4 kids. Goal: validate retention, not scale.

Phase 2 · Tilt

Product earns its share

If kids come back weekly, parents renew, and word of mouth grows — raise subscription pricing, reduce private teaching hours, shift weight toward the product.

Phase 3 · Optional

Path 1 earns permission

Only at real traction — 300+ paying families, stable monthly revenue, organic growth — does Path 1 unlock. Until then it stays a direction, not a deadline.

One more thing worth saying

"Path 2 until Path 1 becomes a reality" is not hedging. It's intelligent runway design. It protects your finances, your energy, your identity, and your optionality — all at once. Many founders in education never fully leave it, and the hybrid becomes the dream. If that's where we end up, that's not a failure mode. That's a full, good ending.
The plan came from your answer — not the other way around.
