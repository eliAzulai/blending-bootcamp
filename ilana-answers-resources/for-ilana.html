Ilana — I took your interview answers and built the next version of WordPets around them. Here's what I heard, what I'm building first, and what I need from you next. Nothing here is final — tell me what's wrong and I'll change it.

What you told me

On the #1 blocker

Motivation.

So the pet comes first. Not as decoration — as the whole reason a kid opens the app tomorrow.
On using it

I would use it with my private students immediately.

Then let's ship a pilot in weeks, not months. Five private students plus our four kids — that's the cohort.
On what NOT to build

Be too bloated and confusing for parents and kids.

Hard scope limit: 3 activity types for launch. Phonics, read‑aloud, spelling. Everything else waits.
On AI speech grading

Record the attempt and I'll review it when I have time — til I can trust it.

The AI never tells a kid pass or fail. It saves the audio and the transcript; you decide.

What I'm building first

1

A pet that's alive on screen

Kids see it, feed it, and watch its mood change. Miss a day — it gets hungry. Practice — it celebrates. The motivation engine.

2

Read‑aloud you review

Kid reads a passage. App records audio + transcript. You review when you have time — no AI grading. Built around your trust.

3

Daily practice that picks itself

Kid opens the app → gets 2–3 short activities from their focus areas → pet celebrates → done. You don't plan it.

4

Spelling practice

Hear the word, type or drag letters to spell. Instant feedback — spelling is safe to auto‑grade. Dolch & Fry lists seeded.

5

Your teacher dashboard

Who practiced, what they did, this week. One‑click review of read‑alouds. 30 min a week, exactly.

6

Seed content from your sources

Phonics words, Dolch sight words, and short decodable passages in the voice of Red Cat Reading, Dr. Seuss, PD Eastman. Not worksheets.

Coming later — not a no

K–8 differentiation · extensions for older kids
Your 4th‑graders who are behind are already at 6–8 reading level — Phase 1 covers them. Explicit K–8 mode comes next, not now.
A liaison between you & parents · auto updates
You asked for this and it's the right instinct — automated weekly parent summaries. Second round, after we know the pilot works.
AI‑generated content · endless new material
Seeded library is enough for the 4‑week pilot. We add AI generation once we know what content shapes actually stick with kids.
Pet room · outfits · evolution · mini‑games
A thin pet is enough to test whether motivation is the lever. If it works, we make the pet bigger. If not, we don't waste the build.

What I need from you

Before we launch

  • Your 5 private students — a list, with ages & what each is working on
  • 3–5 short passages you'd actually want them reading — pick from what you already use
  • Which focus area per kid — phonics, reading, or spelling first

During the pilot

  • 30 min a week reviewing their read‑alouds & adjusting what they practice
  • One honest reaction per week — what worked, what annoyed you
  • Our 4 kids as the first testers before private students see it
Built around your answers, not around my assumptions.
